About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in civil engineering at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, working on transportation systems, with a master's in data science and operations research from Industrial and Systems Engineering. My background before that is in electrical engineering.

Most of my work starts where the data is inconvenient. I assemble and validate transit, freight, and highway corridor datasets, including truck flow data, and turn them into analysis a planner can act on. Recently that has meant truck size and weight policy, corridor level visualization of truck volumes across a statewide network, and open source decision tools that agencies can run themselves rather than commission.

I am interested in research and applied roles where the modeling and the data engineering are the same job.
