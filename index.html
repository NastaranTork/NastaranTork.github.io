About

I am a graduate student in civil engineering at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, working on transportation systems, with a master's in data science and operations research from Industrial and Systems Engineering.

Most of my work starts with messy agency and field data. I assemble and validate transit, freight, and highway corridor datasets, including truck flow data, and turn them into something a planner can act on. Recently that has meant truck size and weight policy, corridor level visualization of truck volumes, and open source tools that public agencies can run themselves.
